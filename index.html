General Nutrition Recommendations

Nutrition guidance

--

🥗

Vegetarian Options

Protein-rich plant-based choices

Breakfast
  • Oats with milk/curd, fruit and nuts
  • Vegetable poha with curd
  • Besan or moong-dal chilla with vegetables
Lunch
  • Dal with roti or brown rice
  • Mixed vegetables with curd
  • Rajma or chana with a balanced portion of rice
Snack
  • Fruit with unsalted nuts
  • Roasted chana
  • Plain yogurt or buttermilk
Dinner
  • Paneer/tofu with vegetables
  • Dal with roti and salad
  • Vegetable soup with a protein source
🍗

Non-Vegetarian Options

Lean animal-protein choices

Breakfast
  • Eggs with whole-grain toast and vegetables
  • Egg omelette with fruit
  • Plain yogurt with oats and nuts
Lunch
  • Grilled chicken with vegetables and roti
  • Fish with rice and salad
  • Chicken curry with vegetables and a moderate rice/roti portion
Snack
  • Boiled eggs with fruit
  • Plain yogurt with nuts
  • Unsweetened milk
Dinner
  • Grilled fish with vegetables
  • Skinless chicken with salad and roti
  • Egg and vegetable preparation with whole grains

Foods to Emphasize

  • Vegetables and seasonal fruits
  • Whole grains and high-fiber foods
  • Pulses, beans and other protein sources
  • Nuts and seeds in sensible portions
  • Water and minimally processed foods

Foods to Limit

  • Sugar-sweetened beverages
  • Highly processed snack foods
  • Frequent deep-fried foods
  • Excess added sugar and refined grains
  • Large portions of calorie-dense foods

Important health information

BMI is a screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. The food suggestions on this page are general educational recommendations, not a diagnosis or individualized medical diet.

Adult BMI Reference

BMI Classification General interpretation
Below 18.5 Underweight Below standard adult reference range
18.5 – 24.9 Normal weight Within standard adult reference range
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight Above standard adult reference range
30.0 or higher Obesity At or above standard adult obesity threshold
